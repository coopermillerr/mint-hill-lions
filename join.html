Get involved

Join the Mint Hill Lions.

Members, volunteers, sponsors and curious neighbors are all welcome. Reach out or just come to a meeting, and we'll take it from there.

How to joinQuestions

Why people join

  • You see the difference right here at home: a kid with new glasses, a full backpack, a gift on Christmas morning.
  • You get to know good people in Mint Hill who show up for each other.
  • You're part of Lions Clubs International, the world's largest service club organization.
  • You can lead. We're rebuilding, and new ideas get a real yes.

What it takes

  • One meeting a month: the 4th Tuesday at 6:30 PM at J-Bird's.
  • Help at events when you can. No minimum hours.
  • Yearly dues, which we'll walk you through at your first meeting.

Not ready to be a member? Volunteering is open to everyone.

Questions

Do I have to be a member to volunteer?

No. Anyone can volunteer at our events. Membership is there if you want a bigger role.

What happens after I reach out?

A club member will get back to you to answer questions and invite you to the next meeting.

How much are dues?

Ask any member at a meeting and we'll walk you through them.

I'm young. Is this club for me?

Yes. Lions clubs need new energy, and we'd love your ideas.
