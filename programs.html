Programs

Where your time and money go.

Lions are known for sight. In Mint Hill we also look after kids, schools and neighbors with low vision.

Sight

Sight for neighbors

We pay for vision screenings, eye exams and glasses for people in our community who can't afford them, and help with dental care when we can.

How to help: Know someone who needs an eye exam? Tell a member or use the contact form.

Kids

Christmas for kids

Every December we quietly deliver gifts to local schools for students whose families are having a hard year.

How to help: Donate gifts or money, or help shop and wrap.

Schools

Back to school

Each year we stuff backpacks with supplies for students at Title I schools so every kid starts the year ready.

How to help: Donate supplies or join us on packing day.

Fundraiser

Annual 5K

Our biggest fundraiser of the year. The money raised goes back into our service projects.

How to help: Run, walk, sponsor or volunteer on race day.

Blind & low vision

Camp Dogwood

We support Camp Dogwood, the North Carolina Lions camp for people who are blind or visually impaired.

How to help: Ask us at a meeting how to get involved.

Blind & low vision

VIP Fishing Tournament

We help with the VIP Fishing Tournament on the Outer Banks, where visually impaired anglers spend time on the water.

How to help: Sign up through NC Lions when registration opens.

Blind & low vision

Braille Trail

We promote and walk the Braille Trail, an accessible trail built for visitors who are blind or have low vision.

How to help: Join us at the next trail walk.

Community

Tent and chair rentals

We rent tents, tables and chairs at club prices. Rentals help pay for everything on this page.

How to help: Book a rental for your next event.

Have an idea for a project?

Members bring ideas to every meeting. If it helps Mint Hill, we want to hear it.
